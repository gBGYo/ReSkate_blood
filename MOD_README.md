# Blood

Give your bails a bloodier aftermath. Blood adds impact spray, droplets, trails and
lingering smears to your skater's falls and slides in Skate.

- Spray and droplets react to impacts, with trails and smears left behind as you slide.
- Choose red, green, blue or pink blood.
- Tune effect size, impact sensitivity, trail density, smear size, bleeding duration
  and how long marks remain. A **Reduced effects** option is also available.
- Use **Clear marks** whenever you want a clean slate. Your settings are saved.

Open the **Blood** plugin page in the ReSkate menu and turn on **Blood effects** to
get started. Effects are off by default.

Blood follows your own skater; other players' falls do not create blood. Effects
pause during loading, replay, Noclip and park editing. Getting back up stops the
bleeding while leaving stains behind; teleporting or changing maps clears them.
